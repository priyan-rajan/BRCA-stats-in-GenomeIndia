BRCA mutations predispose a person to cancer with the risk being as high as 70-80%. While global databases establish the kinds and frequencies of mutations in BRCA, the BRCA mutations of people in the Indian subcontinent is unclear. Based on the newly published Genome India dataset, I analyzed the landscape of BRCA mutations and the frequencies in the Indian population. The data is present in the excel sheet. I covered the story in a blog post here: https://centraldogmatix.substack.com/p/the-story-of-brca-from-mapping-genes
